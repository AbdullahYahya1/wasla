Wasla was built on a real move: one person, twelve years of WhatsApp, a Motorola and an iPhone that already had everything else on it.

Why Wasla

WhatsApp's own move only works on an empty iPhone

The official Android to iPhone transfer runs during setup, so an iPhone you already use has to be reset first. Wasla adds your chats to the iPhone as it is.

The official way

  • Erase the iPhone and set it up again
  • Restore photos, apps and settings afterwards
  • Start over if anything goes wrong

With Wasla

  • Only WhatsApp's data on the iPhone changes
  • Photos, apps and settings stay where they are
  • Undo puts WhatsApp back as it was

How it works

Four stops, with the time left on every one

  1. 1

    Plug in both phones

    Wasla checks the cables, the iPhone's trust prompt and the Mac's free space as you go.

  2. 2

    Copy from Android

    Type the 64-digit backup key, and your chats, contact names and media come over the cable to the Mac.

  3. 3

    Back up and prepare

    A full iPhone backup stays on the Mac as a safety net. Your Android chats are added to WhatsApp's part of it.

  4. 4

    Restore WhatsApp only

    Only WhatsApp's data goes back to the iPhone. Open WhatsApp and scroll up through the years.

Questions

Before you start

Will this erase my iPhone?

No. Only WhatsApp's data is restored. Afterwards the iPhone shows a few setup screens, which is normal after any restore.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Everything moves over the cables and stays on your Mac. Wasla has no account and sends nothing anywhere. The 64-digit key stays in memory and is never saved.

What if something looks wrong afterwards?

Use Undo on the last step. It puts WhatsApp on the iPhone back exactly as it was, from the copy Wasla kept. A full iPhone backup stays on your Mac too.

What about messages that arrive during the move?

Messages that reach the Android phone after its last backup stay on that phone. While the iPhone is in Airplane mode, new messages wait on WhatsApp's servers and arrive after the move.

Is Wasla made by WhatsApp?

No. Wasla is independent, free and open-source software built on wamove. It is not made by or affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta or Apple, and it relies on formats they don't document. Keep your Android phone as it is until you're happy with the result.

Mac says it can't check the app for malicious software

Until Wasla is notarized by Apple, right-click Wasla in Applications, choose Open, then choose Open again. You only need to do this once.
